The Need

Many people with Dementia struggle to recognize loved ones and navigate their own homes, which can cause confusion and stress. Our product helps restore comfort, connection, and independence in daily life.

Millions of families struggle to connect with loved ones when they can't recognize them.
Simple moments like visiting family or navigating a room can become confusing and stressful.
What should feel like home can start to feel unfamiliar.
That is why we created Snapsis. To turn confusion into comfort.
